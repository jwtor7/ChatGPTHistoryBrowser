# Product Visual Prompts

Use these prompts as art direction, then add all final typography in code or a
design tool. Never put real export data, realistic personal details, or a
generated imitation of the product UI into a public visual. When showing the
interface, composite the authentic synthetic screenshot from this repository.

## GitHub hero

> Create a 16:9 GitHub hero illustration at 1600×900 for an independent,
> privacy-focused macOS archive-search utility. Use a restrained editorial
> product style with crisp vector geometry and subtle dimensional lighting.
> Palette: deep forest #173B35, parchment #F4EFE5, archive blue #185F8D, warm
> brass #D8A94D, graphite #20201D, and soft stone #D9D5CB. Reserve the left 38
> percent as calm negative space for separately typeset title and tagline. On
> the right, show a refined generic desktop window with an abstract two-pane
> archive browser, a local folder entering a small on-device index, and a
> magnifying-glass cue; keep every data-flow line visibly inside the device
> boundary. Use non-legible placeholder rows or unmistakably fictional Lantern
> Atlas motifs only. No generated text, real names, prompts, excerpts, emails,
> URLs, filenames, paths, identifiers, or timestamps. No OpenAI or ChatGPT logo,
> swirl, Apple logo, third-party trademark, cloud, upload arrow, robot, face,
> neon cyberpunk, or padlock-dominated stock imagery. Leave Retina-sharp quiet
> space for responsive cropping.

## macOS splash or onboarding illustration

> Create a 3:2 onboarding illustration at 1440×960 for a local archive browser.
> Palette: parchment #F4EFE5, deep forest #173B35, archive blue #185F8D, warm
> brass #D8A94D, cool mist #EAF1F2, and graphite #20201D. Inside a simplified
> laptop boundary, show a calm left-to-right sequence: an extracted folder with
> a small read-only marker, compact stacked local-index cards, then a clean
> search-results panel with a magnifying glass. A single dotted path must never
> leave the computer. Keep the upper-left open for real onboarding copy and the
> lower-left open for a real button. Warm editorial vector style, subtle paper
> texture, gentle macOS-like depth, accessible contrast, and no fake controls.
> Use abstract neutral bars only—no readable user data or generated text. No
> commercial logos, cloud, server rack, upload arrow, Wi-Fi beam, robot, face,
> fingerprint, surveillance imagery, or dramatic lock.

## Product splash page or landing page

> Design a responsive one-page product splash for an independent, open-source
> macOS archive-search utility. Lead with the factual promise “Search your
> exported conversation history on your Mac—locally and read-only,” followed by
> one primary source-build call to action and one link to the privacy model.
> Place the repository’s authentic 1280×720 synthetic product screenshot in the
> hero; do not redraw, replace, or generate the interface. Follow with four
> concise proof blocks: local processing, read-only source, disposable plaintext
> index, and independently synthetic public fixtures. Add a three-step
> “Extract, choose, search” section, a direct plaintext-index warning, current
> status as a macOS source-build MVP, and an independent-project disclaimer.
> Finish with prerequisites, exact build commands, and links to security and
> contribution docs. Visual direction: restrained editorial software design,
> deep forest #173B35, parchment #F4EFE5, archive blue #185F8D, warm brass
> #D8A94D, graphite #20201D, generous whitespace, strong focus states, semantic
> HTML, WCAG AA contrast, reduced-motion support, and no analytics or remote
> assets. No fake testimonials, download counts, performance claims, cloud or
> upload imagery, commercial logos, generated UI, or real user data.

## App icon

> Design a production macOS app icon on a 1024×1024 canvas. Use a native
> rounded-square silhouette, 12 percent safe margin, and two restrained vector
> depth layers. Palette: deep forest #173B35 background, parchment #F4EFE5
> archive cards, archive blue #185F8D, warm brass #D8A94D, and graphite
> #10201E. Combine one bold archive-card or conversation-history stack with a
> simple magnifying-glass cutout; add only a tiny shield notch as a secondary
> local-privacy cue. Limit the design to three large shapes so it remains clear
> at 32 pixels and in monochrome. No letters, words, message content, OpenAI or
> ChatGPT logo or swirl, Apple logo, robot, brain, cloud, fingerprint, keyhole,
> oversized padlock, notification badge, thin linework, or copied iconography.
